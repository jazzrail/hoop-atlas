# Hoop Atlas Glossary

## Court Types

### Full Court

A regulation-length basketball court with two baskets.

### Half Court

A basketball playing area with one basket.

### Multi-use Court

A court shared between basketball and another sport.

### Practice Area

One or more hoops without a full marked basketball court.

## Accessibility

| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1 | Good |
| 2 | Some difficulty |
| 3 | Poor / inaccessible |
| Unknown | Not assessed |
